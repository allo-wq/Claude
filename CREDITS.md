# Credits

## Sounds

Every sound in the add-on is built from public-domain (CC0) recordings published on OpenGameArt. None of it comes from a film, game or comic. `tools/build_sounds.py` shows exactly which recordings are used and how they are cut, pitched down and layered.

| Recording | Author | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Big scary troll sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/big-scary-troll-sounds) | Darsycho | Roar, growls, bonding, boss hurt and death |
| [Monster snarls](https://opengameart.org/content/monster-snarls) | Darsycho | Growls, bite, separation |
| [CC0 Deep Monster Roar](https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-deep-monster-roar) | trazzz123 | Low rumble under the roars, boss death |
| [80 CC0 creature SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx) | rubberduck | Bite, gulp, pain shriek, wild symbiote voice |
| [40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx) | rubberduck | Squelches, tendril, bonding, separation |
| [Swishes Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack) | artisticdude | Tendril lash, spike burst |
| [7 Eating Crunches](https://opengameart.org/content/7-eating-crunches) | StarNinjas | Bite |

All seven are licensed [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Thank you to their authors.

## Art

All textures and models are original, drawn by `tools/build_assets.py`.
