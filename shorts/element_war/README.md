# FIRE vs WATER vs PLANT

A 48-second vertical short (1080×1920, 60 fps, with sound) for **Loop Lab**. The battle,
the visuals and the soundtrack are all generated by code.

**▶ [`element_war_short.mp4`](element_war_short.mp4)**, cover frame: [`cover.png`](cover.png)

## What happens

240 particles start in three armies: 80 flames, 80 water drops and 80 leaves. Each one chases the
nearest thing it beats and runs from the nearest thing that beats it. Touch your prey and it joins your side:

- 🔥 **Fire burns Plant**
- 🌿 **Plant drinks Water**
- 💧 **Water puts out Fire**

Nobody is strongest, so the lead swings back and forth until one element owns the whole arena.

**This battle (seed 231):**
- **0:07:** Plant is down to **a single leaf**, and Fire surges to 216.
- **0:36:** Water reaches **237 of 240**, three conversions away from winning. It puts out the last flame…
- …but Fire was the only thing keeping Plant in check. **"…AND PLANT DRINKS WATER!"** The two surviving
  leaves convert all 237 drops in about four seconds.
- **Finish:** the screen reads "PLANT TAKES OVER! Came back from a single leaf! (Water was 3 away from winning)",
  followed by "Did you pick Plant?" and the Loop Lab end card.

It isn't scripted. `render.py search` simulated 400 battles and ranked them by drama (comeback size,
how close the loser came, length). Seed 231 came out on top.

**Sound:** a 120 BPM synth track (A minor) adds layers as the battle heats up, and every conversion has its own
sound: a crackle when fire wins, a bloop when water wins, a pluck when plant wins. Elements that are wiped out
power down, and the finish has a slow-motion riser, an impact and a major-key fanfare. Mastered to about −14 LUFS.

## Re-render / new battles

Requires Python 3, `numpy`, `pillow`, `pycairo` and `ffmpeg`.

```bash
python3 render.py search --count 400          # rank battles by drama
python3 render.py still --times 2,20,final,end # preview PNGs
python3 render.py render --seed 231            # full MP4
```

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `sim.py` | The battle: chase/flee steering, collisions, conversions, recorded per frame |
| `render.py` | Vector icons, HUD, effects, story text, soundtrack and encode |
| `../common.py` | Shared Loop Lab toolbox (bloom, text, slow-mo, confetti, synth, mastering) |
| `../brand.py` | The Loop Lab end card |
