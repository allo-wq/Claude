Method

Investigate carefully, verifiably and lawfully

Tools find data; method turns it into findings others can trust.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Write down the question you are answering, your legal basis and what is out of scope.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Gather from open sources, passively where possible. Archive pages as you find them.

  3. 03

    Process

    Normalise and deduplicate. Hash and timestamp files; never edit originals.

  4. 04

    Analyse

    Corroborate across independent sources. Rate source reliability and your confidence.

  5. 05

    Report

    Cite every source, capture date and method so someone else can reproduce your work.

OPSEC checklist

Tick these off before you start. Saved in this browser.

    Ethics & law

    • Have a legitimate purpose and stay within the law and each platform's terms of service.
    • Collect only what you need. Personal data you don't gather can't leak or be misused.
    • Never harass, stalk, dox or intimidate. Findings about people carry real-world consequences.
    • Treat breach data with caution. Possessing or using it can be unlawful depending on where you are.
    • Verify before you publish. Misidentification harms innocent people; state your confidence.

    Further reading: the Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations.
